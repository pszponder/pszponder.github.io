# About Me

Hi, I’m Piotr Szponder — a data engineer passionate about transforming raw data into meaningful insights and building systems that create real impact. My work is driven by a simple focus: identifying and addressing customer problems through technology.

Right now, I’m especially interested in the world of AI and Machine Learning — exploring how intelligent systems can be applied to practical challenges, improve decision-making, and unlock new opportunities for businesses and people alike.

For me, technology is both a craft and a journey: every pipeline, model, or application is valuable only if it solves a real problem. Through this blog, I share what I’m learning, experiment with ideas, and reflect on how data, AI, and software can better serve end users.
